I'm a Modelling Specialist in Pharmacometrics at Novo Nordisk, where I spend most of my time on R package development, standardization and automation, and AI. Part of my work is also dedicated to [FeRx](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME), an open-source nonlinear mixed-effects modelling engine.

I have a PhD in Medical Science from the [Centre of Ethics, Law and Mental Health](https://www.gu.se/en/celam) at University of Gothenburg, and I'm an affiliated researcher in the [FORevidence](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/organisations/forevidence/) unit at Lund University. See the full [CV](/cv/) for details.

Sometimes I take photos, and sometimes they even end up in the [gallery](/gallery/).
